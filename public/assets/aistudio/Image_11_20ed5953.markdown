## Brand & Style

This design system evokes an intimate, tactile sanctuary—a digitized keepsake reminiscent of a treasured linen-bound scrapbook. It fuses the nostalgic warmth of vintage paper goods with the airy, curated visual vocabulary of modern stationery and Pinterest aesthetics. 

The emotional signature is calming, safe, reflective, and softly indulgent. The visual style merges **Tactile / Skeuomorphic subtleties** with **Minimal editorial elegance**:
- Subtle paper textures, tactile polaroid framing, and muted washi tape overlays evoke tangible stationery without visual clutter.
- Low-contrast warmth replaces harsh clinical whites, crafting a cozy room for unguarded vulnerability and daily memories.
- Expressive editorial typefaces contrast effortlessly with clean, legible structures and whimsical handwritten accents.

## Colors

The palette balances warm cream foundations with romantic dusty rose touches and antique botanical accents, maintaining calm legibility across long-form journaling sessions.

- **Primary (`#C98D9B`) — Dusty Rose**: Applied to key actions, selected states, and pivotal navigation accents. Anchors the interface with mature romance.
- **Secondary (`#F5DDE3`) — Blush Pink**: Used for soft container fills, card highlights, and mood tags.
- **Tertiary (`#CCD8C3`) — Muted Sage**: An earthy botanical companion used for positive reflections, organic badges, and plant stickers.
- **Canvas / Background (`#F7F0E5`) — Warm Cream**: The core base layer across the mobile interface, replacing raw white with the tactile gentleness of parchment.
- **Surface Neutrals**:
  - Paper Surface: `#FFFAF3` (Clean Journal Cream for primary content cards).
  - Keepsake Tint: `#F9E9ED` (Soft Baby Pink for highlighted scrapbook entries and quotes).
  - Structural Divider / Tape: `#E9DDCB` (Vintage Beige).
- **Secondary Accent**: `#E5DDF0` (Soft Lavender for dream logs and contemplative prompts).
- **Ink & Copy**:
  - Dominant Body & Headers: `#4A3B36` (Deep Cocoa for soft, high-contrast readability).
  - Secondary / Timestamps: `#76635B` (Cocoa Brown for muted meta tags and borders).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes romantic literary flair with crisp, effortless legibility:

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display)**: Infuses diary prompts, entry titles, and month headings with poetic grace.
- **Narrative & System Copy (DM Sans)**: Used for body text, settings, and navigation. Its low-contrast geometry guarantees effortless reading, preventing eye strain during lengthy late-night writing.
- **Handwritten Notes & Asides**: Decorative callouts, margins, stickers, and dates leverage a script style (e.g., Caveat or Dancing Script) rendered at 16–18px with a 3° to 5° rotation to mimic spontaneous pen annotations on parchment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model simulates an open-bound mobile notebook. It balances structured card feeds with spontaneous collage compositions.

- **Grid & Safe Margins**: Standard mobile screens employ a fluid 4-column layout anchored with `1.25rem` (`margin`) outer edge paddings. Tablet and expanded views transition into an asymmetric 8-column layout mimicking dual facing scrapbook pages.
- **Breathing Room**: Vertical pacing is generous. Distinct journal days are separated by `space-xl` (`2rem`) to maintain calm, uncluttered pacing.
- **Dynamic Offsets**: Scrapbook elements (polaroids, stickers, and washi tags) employ subtle negative margin overlaps (e.g., `-0.5rem`) and gentle rotation transforms (`-2deg` to `+2deg`) to break the rigid digital boundary while preserving tap target standards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons clinical drop shadows in favor of **warm ambient diffusion** and **layered stationery physics**:

- **Paper Stack Elevation**: Depth is primarily communicated through tonal contrast. The Warm Cream background (`#F7F0E5`) holds Clean Journal Cream cards (`#FFFAF3`), which gently rise using an ambient, warm tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 18px -2px rgba(118, 99, 91, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 59, 54, 0.04);`
- **Washi Tape Elements**: Washi accents appear semi-translucent (`opacity: 0.85; mix-blend-mode: multiply;`) spanning top edges of cards or polaroids, sporting feathered paper-cut edges.
- **Floating Prompts & Overlays**: Modals and floating action elements cast a deeper, warm embrace:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 59, 54, 0.12);`
- **Polaroid Framing**: Photo memories feature a thicker bottom rim (typical of vintage instant film), resting flat on the card canvas with a faint `1px` inner boundary of `rgba(118, 99, 91, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to evoke soft, safe stationery corners:

- **Journal Cards & Surfaces**: Standard container radii sit comfortably at `1rem` (`16px`), scaling up to `1.5rem` (`24px`) for hero prompts and entry sheets.
- **Buttons & Chips**: Interactive mood badges and small chips use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to invite gentle touch.
- **Washi Tape Accents**: Taped edges feature precise, straight cuts or subtle jagged zigzag masks, contrasted intentionally against the rounded paper cards they secure.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (New Entry / Save)**: Filled in Dusty Rose (`#C98D9B`) with Deep Cocoa (`#4A3B36`) or white text, styled with full pill borders (`9999px`) and a subtle warm button shadow. Active states scale down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Action**: Background of Blush Pink (`#F5DDE3`), text in Deep Cocoa (`#4A3B36`), flat borderless design with `space-sm` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding.
- **Ghost / Text Button**: Transparent base with Cocoa Brown text, underlined by a soft watercolor stroke on tap.

### Paper Cards & Entry Containers
- Surface rendered in Clean Journal Cream (`#FFFAF3`) or Soft Baby Pink (`#F9E9ED`) with `1rem` rounded corners and warm ambient shadow.
- Optional decorative washi tape strip at the top-center (tilted between `-2deg` and `+2deg`), colored in Vintage Beige (`#E9DDCB`) or Muted Sage (`#CCD8C3`).

### Mood Badges & Chips
- Soft pastel pills rendered in Muted Sage, Soft Lavender, or Blush Pink.
- Lettering set in `label-sm` DM Sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em`).
- Unselected states display transparent backgrounds with a delicate 1px border in Cocoa Brown (`rgba(118, 99, 91, 0.2)`).

### Polaroid Image Displays
- Asymmetrical paper borders: `space-sm` on left, top, and right; `space-xl` on bottom to resemble classic instant film.
- Lower margin holds handwritten-style captions or timestamps in Deep Cocoa script.

### Input Fields & Text Areas
- Clean, open writing pads. Eliminates rigid box borders, using subtle horizontal dotted lines tinted in `#E9DDCB` (spaced to match `body-lg` line-heights) to recreate lined journal pages.
- Placeholder copy set in Cocoa Brown at 50% opacity in Playfair Display italic.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Elements
- Custom rounded checkboxes with a hand-drawn checkmark aesthetic. Checked state fills with Dusty Rose (`#C98D9B`), accompanied by a gentle bouncy spring transition.
